# NeuroCafe Review Checklist

Use this checklist for full reviews of NeuroCafe social-network artifacts.

## ТЗ Fit

- The artifact explains why the social network exists inside the app.
- The social network is positioned as practice/community progress, not as a generic post feed.
- Required outputs are represented: UX audit, UX solution presentation, prototype decisions, launch communication, and implementation recommendations when relevant.
- Mobile and desktop considerations are separated when the artifact covers both.
- The result is realistic for staged implementation.

## Core User Journey

- First entry answers: what is this section, why should I care, what should I do now.
- A user can reach the first meaningful action quickly: comment, response, publication, support, or repost.
- The user sees immediate feedback after a meaningful action.
- Comments, reposts, and publications are tied to streaks, neuro rewards, badges, or challenge progress.
- Empty states provide seeded examples and first actions.
- Navigation among feed, challenges, friends, and launch/announcement material is understandable.

## Engagement And Retention

- Daily goal is visible and concrete.
- Streaks are motivating but not punitive.
- Personal challenges explain why the goal was chosen.
- Friend mechanics encourage support and shared practice, not only referral pressure.
- Rewards are tied to meaningful behavior, not passive opens or repeated low-effort actions.
- The system protects quality: unique materials, limits on repeated actions, examples of good responses, reporting, and moderation.

## UI And Accessibility

- Primary CTA is clear on each screen.
- Secondary and service actions are not confused with user actions.
- Text hierarchy supports scanning on mobile.
- Buttons have adequate tap targets and do not overflow.
- Contrast is sufficient for important text and controls.
- Content does not overlap at mobile, tablet, desktop, or wide desktop widths.
- HTML/app prototypes are visually checked at mobile, tablet, desktop, and wide desktop widths when tooling is available.
- Russian labels are natural, specific, and consistent.
- Visual reward elements do not obscure the core task.

## Launch Readiness

- Email, banner, and push explain user value and first action.
- Launch copy does not promise features that are absent in the MVP.
- Instructor/seed posts are included or planned so the community does not feel empty.
- First-week activation path is explicit.
- Analytics events cover first entry, first response, first post, support, repost, challenge join, friend invite, reward feedback, and return.
- Team/admin copy controls are not visible as user-facing interface unless the screen is explicitly an internal launch dashboard.

## Prototype And Handoff

- Prototype covers happy path and at least one unclear/empty state.
- New mechanics include acceptance criteria for developers.
- Stage 2 deliverables include the acceptance matrix required in `SKILL.md`.
- Review findings cite evidence with local file paths and line numbers when source files were inspected.
- Risks are prioritized by launch impact.
- Each recommended change has an owner or next step.
- Deferred ideas are separated from MVP requirements.
